The concerning part is that I've started to trust it over the clock in the kitchen, which is correct, which means I've chosen a beautiful lie over an ugly fact, which is, I'm told, the basis of most long-term relationships.

No news otherwise. The second hand ticks in a way that sounds like it's counting something other than seconds.

Formal notice regarding dust: I have concluded, after three weekends of investigation, that dust does not accumulate. It arrives. There is a difference, and the difference is intent.

I swept the same rectangle of hallway on Saturday and again on Sunday and produced identical quantities, which is not how depletion works. Nothing in nature refills that precisely except tides, and tides at least have the decency to be governed by a moon we can see.

I am not alleging a conspiracy. I am alleging a schedule. Someone is running a route through my apartment and they are very good at their job.
