The ketchup packet in my glovebox has been there since a drive-thru in 2019 and it has not changed in any measurable way, which I find more alarming than if it had.

Food does things over time. That is the arrangement. This packet has declined to participate. It is still red, still taut, still legible, still prepared to serve. It has outlasted two cars and a relationship and it is waiting for a hot dog that is never going to arrive.

I took it inside and put it on the counter and looked at it for a while. Then I put it back, because the glovebox is clearly where it has chosen to conduct its long project, and I don't want to be the one who interrupts.

Made meatloaf. It was fine. I want to address, however, the fact that meatloaf is the only food named after its container and has never been renamed despite everyone being slightly uncomfortable with it.

Nobody looks at it with joy. People look at it with recognition, which is a different and heavier emotion. It arrives at the table already carrying a childhood, usually someone else's, and you eat it under supervision from the past.
